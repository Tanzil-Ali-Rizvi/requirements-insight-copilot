# Seller handling policy

*Illustrative policy written for this case study. It is not Olist's actual policy.*

## Handoff SLA

Sellers must hand each parcel to the carrier by the shipping limit shown on the order. The shipping limit is set when the payment is approved and is typically 6 days later (90% of orders within 9 days). The handoff time is the carrier's first scan.

## Late handoff definition

A handoff is late when the carrier's first scan is after the shipping limit. For multi-item orders, the latest shipping limit on the order applies.

## Alerts

Sellers receive one reminder 24 hours before the shipping limit if the parcel has not been scanned. The order is also added to the seller-operations at-risk queue.

## Scorecard

Seller operations publishes a weekly scorecard of late-handoff rate for every seller with at least 30 delivered orders.

## Watchlist and escalation

A seller whose late-handoff rate is above 15% for two consecutive weeks joins the watchlist. Watchlist sellers get a call from seller operations within 5 business days and an improvement plan. A seller still above 15% after four more weeks may have new listings paused, with approval from the Head of Operations.

## Exemptions

Carrier pickup failures confirmed by the carrier, public holidays and declared strikes do not count as late handoffs.
